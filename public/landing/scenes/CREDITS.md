# Hero scene footage credits

All four clips are free stock footage under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
(free for commercial and personal use, no attribution required, self-hosting permitted).
Each was trimmed to an 8.5 s seamless loop (the last second crossfades into the first),
transcoded to H.264 / 1280×720 / 30 fps, muted, and given a poster JPG from frame 0.
The scene order and labels live in `src/components/landing/scenes.ts`.
